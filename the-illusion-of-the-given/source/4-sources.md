# SOURCES AND FURTHER READING

*Texts Referred to in This Article*

## Buddhist Sources

- **Nāgārjuna**, *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* (*Fundamental Verses on the Middle Way*): chapter 5 on space; verses 24.14 and 24.18.
- **Candrakīrti**, *Madhyamakāvatāra* (*Entering the Middle Way*), chapter 6: the critique of Mind-Only, the hungry ghost and the eye disease, and correct and mistaken conventions from the world's point of view.
- **Tsongkhapa**, *Illumination of the Thought* (*dGongs pa rab gsal*), his commentary on *Entering the Middle Way*.
- **Vasubandhu**, *Viṃśatikā* (*Twenty Verses*), verses 3–4; *Abhidharmakośa* (*Treasury of Abhidharma*), chapters 3 and 4.
- **Asaṅga**, *Mahāyānasaṃgraha* (*Compendium of the Mahāyāna*).
- **Dharmakīrti**, *Santānāntarasiddhi* (*Proof of Other Mental Streams*); **Ratnakīrti**, *Santānāntaradūṣaṇa* (*Refutation of Other Mental Streams*).
- *The Questions of King Milinda* (*Milindapañha*): the simile of the lamp burning through the night.
- *Vimalakīrti Sūtra* (*Vimalakīrtinirdeśa*), chapter 1: the purification of the buddha-field.
- *Avataṃsaka Sūtra* (*Flower Ornament Scripture*); **Dushun**, *Contemplation of the Dharmadhātu*; **Fazang**, *Essay on the Golden Lion*; **Chengguan**, commentaries on the *Avataṃsaka Sūtra*.
- **Zhiyi**, *Mohe zhiguan* (*Great Calming and Contemplation*).
- **Longchenpa**, *Precious Treasury of the Basic Space of Phenomena* (*Chos dbyings mdzod*).
- **Ju Mipham**, *Beacon of Certainty* (*Nges shes sgron me*). See John Whitney Pettit, *Mipham's Beacon of Certainty* (Wisdom Publications, 2002).

## Philosophy and Science

- **Immanuel Kant**, *Critique of Pure Reason* (1781; 2nd ed. 1787).
- **Ludwig Wittgenstein**, *Philosophical Investigations* (1953), §§241–242.
- **Jakob von Uexküll**, *A Foray into the Worlds of Animals and Humans* (1934; English translation 2010).
- **Karen Barad**, *Meeting the Universe Halfway* (Duke University Press, 2007).
- **Carlo Rovelli**, "Relational Quantum Mechanics," *International Journal of Theoretical Physics* (1996); *Helgoland* (2021).
- **Emily Adlam and Carlo Rovelli**, "Information Is Physical: Cross-Perspective Links in Relational Quantum Mechanics," *Philosophy of Physics* (2023).
- **Hugh Everett III**, "'Relative State' Formulation of Quantum Mechanics," *Reviews of Modern Physics* (1957).
- **David Wallace**, *The Emergent Multiverse* (Oxford University Press, 2012).
