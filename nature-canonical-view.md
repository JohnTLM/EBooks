# The Governing View of "Nature" (canonical; all chapters align to this)

1. **Emptiness has no intrinsic nature.** It is the absence of any fixing. It is not called open, closed, luminous or ground in itself.

2. **Openness and contrivance are modes of seeing, not properties of emptiness.** The same unfixed appearing is met as open when the habit of fixing is not operating, and as contrived when it is. The difference lies in the seeing, not in emptiness and not in a seer behind it.

3. **Appearing is open because it is dependent.** Openness describes how dependent appearing meets when unfixed. It does not explain why appearing occurs, and it is not a ground that produces appearing.

4. **Practice is undoing, and undoing requires clear knowing.** The path is not adding a result. It is undoing the habit of fixing, including the habit of analysis, which is a habit of division. Non-action without clarity is dullness, not freedom. Practice is still practice, but its aim is release of effort, not achievement.

5. **Freedom is not produced or caused.** It is not a result of causes, and it is not an uncaused base. Causal regularity holds conventionally. The demand for an intrinsic causal power behind it has no object.

6. **No foundation, no hierarchy.** Conditions are not a ground beneath appearances. Their alignment is the appearing, shown without a layer, base or intermediary.

7. **Conventional division stands.** Parts are designated by convention to serve function. The error is taking designations to have their own ground. Conventional functioning is what emptiness looks like when it is not reified, not a second reality beneath it. Denying division as ultimate would itself install an essence.

8. **Recognition is not achievement, but recognition is not passivity.** Effortlessness held as an accomplishment becomes another fixed object. Recognition still requires the clear knowing of item 4.

9. **Negation is non-implicative.** It removes the fixing and installs no positive ground. The implicative kind is the disease returning.
